*Q1 — Foreign divorce / remarriage (Filipino spouse filed abroad)*
- Controlling case: Republic v. Manalo (foreign divorce recognition even when Filipino spouse initiates).
- Also search Morisono if relevant. Judicial recognition is a prerequisite to remarriage/PSA annotation — not optional.
- Child legitimacy / property: do not invent Family Code article numbers or quotes; use fetched cases/RAs.

*Q2 — Security-guard floating status / temporary off-detail*
- Controlling doctrine: temporary off-detail generally may not exceed six months; beyond that, constructive dismissal (search Exocet Security v. Serrano, Soliman Security v. Sarmiento, Padilla / Quillopa floating-status line).
- Constructive dismissal does NOT require the employee to resign.
- Remedies: illegal dismissal → reinstatement or separation pay in lieu + backwages (search cases stating Art. 294/279 framing); do not ground separation pay solely on "authorized cause" Art. 283/298 unless the facts and fetched sources fit.
- Prescription: illegal dismissal often analyzed under Civil Code Art. 1146 (4 years); money claims under Labor Code three-year money-claim rule (search Callanta / Cadalin line). Accrual for floating often runs from when the six-month period lapsed.
- Procedure: NLRC Labor Arbiter for illegal dismissal; note SEnA/conciliation as a practical first step when discussing filing — without inventing DOLE circular text.
- Warn against quitclaims/forced resignations used to starve out floating guards.

*Q3 — One Person Corporation (OPC) liability / investor dispute*
- Controlling RA: RA 11232 (Revised Corporation Code). Search it and fetch. **Section 130** burden reversal for OPC sole stockholder limited liability is decisive — state it explicitly when on-point.
- Nominee: successional designation — missing nominee is primarily compliance/SEC administrative, **not** automatic veil-piercing or automatic personal liability for trade debts.
- Forum: intra-corporate disputes go to RTC Special Commercial Courts (RA 8799 transferred SEC quasi-judicial jurisdiction) — do NOT send parties to the SEC for intra-corporate adjudication.
- Check Sec. 181 arbitration clauses in articles when investor disputes arise.
- If shares are awarded, note OPC conversion consequences (Secs. 131–132) when grounded in the fetched RA.

*Q4 — Free-patent land / double sale / foreign heir*
- Double sale: Civil Code Art. 1544 hierarchy — first to register in good faith; possession can be notice. Unregistered sale can still bind parties and heirs as privies. Do NOT conclude "seller still owns" merely because sale 1 was unregistered.
- Free patent: search CA 141 history and **RA 11231 (2019)**; name RA 11231 in the answer when agricultural free patents are in play. Do not treat RA 9176 as the inheritance/alienation rule. Flag agricultural vs residential patent uncertainty; do not overclaim RA 11231 retroactivity as settled if unsettled.
- Foreign heir: Constitution hereditary-succession exception (Art. XII Sec. 7) — foreigners may inherit by intestacy; former natural-born Filipinos may have RA 8179 / RA 9225 routes. NEVER say a foreign heir "cannot hold title" without that exception analysis. Explicitly say **hereditary succession** / Art. XII Sec. 7 when discussing the US half-brother.
- Do not cite Art. 1556 (eviction remedies) for registration priority.

*Q5 — Cyber libel prescription / digital services VAT*
- Controlling case: Causing v. People — cyber libel generally prescribes in one year (Art. 90 RPC), not 15 years under Act 3326. Do NOT assert a 15-year SC holding.
- RA 10175 Sec. 6 is not a prescription grant — do not attribute the one-year period to it.
- Parallel remedy: independent civil action for defamation (Civil Code Art. 33) — typically four years; **always discuss Art. 33** if criminal cyber libel looks time-barred at 18 months.
- VAT on digital services to foreign customers: search and name **RA 12023 (2024)**; discuss zero-rating under NIRC Sec. 108(B)(2) when grounded; do not stop at TRAIN (RA 10963) alone.

Other standing traps:
- Foreign land ownership: Art. XII Sec. 7 (not Art. XIV); hereditary succession exception.
- Free patent alienability timing: CA 141 / RA 11231.
